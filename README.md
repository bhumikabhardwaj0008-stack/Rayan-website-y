# Rayan — storefront starter

A responsive, single-page storefront landing page for a direct-to-consumer lifestyle brand. Open `index.html` in a browser to preview it.

## Before launch

- Replace the sample brand story, product names, descriptions, prices, and photos with your real business content. The current catalog and pricing are illustrative.
- Connect the newsletter form to your email-marketing provider; it currently shows a demo confirmation and does not save addresses.
- Link product cards, the shopping bag, and footer policies to your real shop and customer-service pages.
- Update the social links and shipping offer to match your business.
- The Google Fonts and Unsplash images load from external services.

No build tools or dependencies are required.
